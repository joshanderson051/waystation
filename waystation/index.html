What is Port Solace?At a glance

Port Solace is a free space station builder that plays in your browser and keeps running on the clock while the tab is closed. You command a way station on the edge of interstellar space with one docking collar and 6,000 credits, dock freighters and trade their cargo, keep the power on, and send expeditions to chart eight sectors. A few weeks later the collar has grown into a galactic gate the whole reach routes through, and then you do it again around another star.

What you doSix systems · one console

Dock the traffic Ops

Freighters pull up to your bays for fuel, repairs and a hot meal, and pay on departure. One bay, one ship at a time; every bay needs an open side for the approach.

Trade the cargo Trade

Ships sell what their home world exports and buy what it imports. Set Buy and Sell per good, hold ore for a refinery, and ride the booms.

Tap the banks Income

Every module earns credits by the minute into its own bank. A glowing coin means it is ready: tap it and watch the credits fly home. Banks keep filling while you are away.

Build on the clock Timers

Modules take real time to come online, from ninety seconds to four hours by tier, and upgrades take longer still. Queue a build, go live your life, come back to a brighter station. Antimatter skips the wait.

Venture out Expeditions

Send surveys from your docks to any of eight sectors. They come back with cargo, charts, Antimatter and discoveries for the Registry: ship classes, species, artifacts.

Claim new worlds Galaxy

Clear rings of unclaimed space around your hub as the station grows, then found the next station in one of eight sectors: dust storms, an asteroid belt, an ocean, a ringed giant, the violet gate. One wallet across all of them.

Come back forThere is always something cooking

Missions

Fifty goals in seven chapters, three live at a time. Finish one, come back and claim the credits, Antimatter and rank it pays.

Achievements

Twenty-four lifetime achievements in bronze, silver and gold, from your first fifty ships to the whole Registry, each tier paying Antimatter.

Daily reward

A seven-day streak of credits, crates and Antimatter, with a livery token on day seven. Miss a day and you can mend the streak.

Commander rank

Ensign to Admiral of the Reach. Ranks are earned across every sector and grant real powers: collect all banks at once, a fourth mission, a bonus on every rate.

The Registry

Twenty-four ship classes, twelve alien species and sixteen artifacts to find, with lore for each. Completing a set pays Antimatter and a title.

Territory

Every station starts on a small plot. Buy the next ring of unclaimed space, wait for the crews to clear it, and the station grows outward, ring by ring, in every sector.

Premium currency

Antimatter

Earned by missions, daily rewards and expeditions, and sold in the Store. Spend it to skip a build, call an expedition home, open a sector before you have the charts, or dress the station in a sector livery. The game is free to play; nothing in it needs Antimatter.

Purchases are handled securely by Stripe Checkout and charged in US dollars. Items arrive on your station as soon as the payment goes through.

Saves

Your commander profile saves to your browser every few seconds and when you leave the tab. Timers run on the clock, so builds finish and banks fill while you are gone.

One profile per browser, every sector inside it. Export and import it from Settings, or sign in to keep a cloud save and pick up on another device. Without an account nothing leaves your device.

Screens

Built for a desktop browser at 1280 px and wider with WebGL on; a landscape tablet works, and on a phone the bridge folds into a bottom dock with the panels as sheets.

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Reduced-motion settings are honoured, and the effects tier steps down on slower machines.

QuestionsPort Solace, answered

What kind of game is this?

A space station builder that plays in the browser and keeps running on the clock while the tab is closed. You start with one docking collar and 6,000 credits, and a few weeks later you have a port the whole reach routes through.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free to play, and nothing in it needs Antimatter. Antimatter is earned by missions, daily rewards and expeditions and is also sold in the Store, where purchases are handled by Stripe Checkout and charged in US dollars.

Do I need an account?

No. Your commander profile saves to your browser. Sign in to keep a cloud save and pick up on another device; without an account nothing leaves your device.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It is built for a desktop browser at 1280 px and wider with WebGL on; a landscape tablet works, and on a phone the bridge folds into a bottom dock with the panels as sheets.

Is Port Solace part of Build the Ages?

Yes. Port Solace is listed with the other games at buildtheages.com and also plays at space.buildtheages.com, and one account signs in to all of them.
